    Description: This course introduces the modern methods of analysis in partial differential equations. Emphasis
    is placed on the theory of existence, uniqueness and stability of solutions to boundary-value problems and initial-value problems.
    This theory is developed in the context of the prototypical PDE arising in mathematical physics (such as transport, wave,
    heat/diffusion,     Laplace/Poisson equations). We'll study in particular the theory of distributions; elliptic problems in
    Hilbert-Sobolev spaces using the variational formulation; parabolic problems; hyperbolic problems and energy methods.
    The course assumes that students are familiar with undergraduate level ODE and PDE.
    Note: Modern Real Analysis (Measure Theory, Hilbert Spaces, L^p-theory, etc) at a first-year graduate level is assumed. 
    Material from Math 623 and Math 624 (or equivalents) are a prerequisite.
